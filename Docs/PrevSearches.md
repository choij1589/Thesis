# Previous Searches for Charged Higgs and Light Pseudoscalar

Organized by collider/experiment era, then by decay channel within each.
The bosonic decays H+ → Wh, WH, WA were called the **"forgotten channels"** in Ref. 2103.07484 (Bahl, Stefaniak, Wittbrodt, JHEP 06, 2021, 183), noting that these channels often dominate over fermionic decays but had never been experimentally probed until LHC Run 2.

## 2. LEP

Production via pair production at √s up to 209 GeV.

| Channel | Collaborations | 95% CL Exclusion | arXiv | Year |
|---------|---------------|-------------------|-------|------|
| H+ → τν, cs̄, W*A | ALEPH, DELPHI, L3, OPAL (combined) | m_H+ > 80 GeV (Type-II), > 72.5 GeV (Type-I, m_A > 12 GeV) | 1301.6065 | 2013 |

**Notes:**

- Type-I includes the bosonic decay H+ → W*A (searched by DELPHI and OPAL).

## 6. Excluded Mass Points Relevant to This Analysis

Our analysis parameter space: **m_H+ = 80–160 GeV, m_A = 15–75 GeV** (including off-shell W*).

### 6.1 Direct constraints on H+ → W±A → W± μ+μ-

**CMS (1905.07453):** On-shell W only (m_H+ − m_A ≥ 85 GeV).
- Scenario 1: m_H+ = m_A + 85 GeV, scanning m_A = 15–75 GeV in ~95 points. Diagonal line from (100, 15) to (160, 75).
- Scenario 2: m_H+ = 160 GeV fixed, same 95 m_A values.
- **190 total (m_H+, m_A) points**, all on-shell.
- Limits: B_cascade < (1.9–8.6) × 10⁻⁶.

**ATLAS (2304.14247):** Also on-shell W only.
- Three discrete m_H+ values: **120, 140, 160 GeV**.
  - m_H+ = 120 GeV: 13 m_a points, m_a = 15.1–29.5 GeV
  - m_H+ = 140 GeV: 25 m_a points, m_a = 15.1–43.7 GeV
  - m_H+ = 160 GeV: 31 m_a points, m_a = 15.1–70.2 GeV
- **69 total (m_H+, m_a) points**.
- Limits: B_cascade < (0.9–3.9) × 10⁻⁶.

### 6.2 What is NOT covered by existing searches

1. **Off-shell W* region (m_H+ − m_A < m_W ~ 80 GeV):** Neither CMS nor ATLAS probed any off-shell mass points. This includes:
   - All points with m_H+ = 80–100 GeV
   - m_H+ = 120 GeV, m_A > 40 GeV
   - m_H+ = 140 GeV, m_A > 60 GeV
   - Any (m_H+, m_A) with splitting < 85 GeV not on the CMS diagonal

2. **Low m_H+ region (80–100 GeV):** CMS starts at 100 GeV; ATLAS at 120 GeV. No coverage below.

3. **Intermediate m_H+ values:** ATLAS only probes {120, 140, 160}. CMS only the diagonal + m_H+=160 line.

### 6.3 Indirect constraints on our parameter space

- **LEP:** m_H+ > 72.5 GeV (Type-I); m_A > 93.4 GeV in MSSM (but relaxed in general 2HDM).
- **B-factories:** Constrain m_A < ~10 GeV via Υ radiative decays (below our m_A range).
- **H+ → τν (ATLAS 140 fb⁻¹):** Weak in Type-I at large tanβ due to cot²β suppression.
- **H+ → cs̄ (ATLAS 140 fb⁻¹):** Weak in Type-I at large tanβ where B(H+→WA) dominates.
- **Standalone A → μμ (CMS scouting, 137 fb⁻¹):** Probes ggF/bb-associated, not the t → H+b cascade.

## Summary: What Remains Unexplored

1. **H+ → W±A → W± μ+μ-**: Two searches exist (CMS 35.9 fb⁻¹ on-shell only; ATLAS 139 fb⁻¹ on-shell only). The **off-shell W* regime**, where custodial symmetry naturally places much of the viable parameter space, remains unexplored.
2. **τν and cs/cb channels** are insensitive to the Type-I large-tanβ regime where H+ → WA dominates — this is why it was called the "forgotten channel."
3. **Standalone A → μμ searches** probe different production modes and do not constrain the t → H+b → W+Ab cascade.
4. The bosonic decay is the **unique discovery mode** for Type-I 2HDM at large tanβ with a light pseudoscalar.
5. **This thesis** performs the CMS search with Run 2 + Run 3 (2022–2023) = **200 fb⁻¹**, extending coverage to off-shell W* decays for the first time.
