\chapter{Results}
\label{ch:results}

This chapter presents the statistical interpretation of the search. The signal branching ratio used for the limit setting is defined first, followed by the observed and expected upper limits obtained from the dimuon mass templates described in Section~\ref{ssec:templates}.

\section{Signal branching ratio}

The signal branching ratio is defined as
\begin{equation}
\mathcal{B}_{\text{sig}} =
\mathcal{B}(\PQt \to \PHp\PQb)
\mathcal{B}(\PHp \to \PWp\PA)
\mathcal{B}(\PA \to \Pgmp\Pgmm).
\label{eq:bsig_results}
\end{equation}
The corresponding signal cross section is
\begin{equation}
\sigma_{\text{sig}} =
2 \, \sigma(\Pp\Pp \to \ttbar) \, \mathcal{B}_{\text{sig}},
\label{eq:sigxsec_results}
\end{equation}
where the factor of two accounts for either the top quark or antiquark decaying through the charged Higgs boson cascade.

\section{Observed and expected upper limits}

Upper limits at 95\% confidence level are set on $\mathcal{B}_{\text{sig}}$ using the modified frequentist CL$_s$ method described in Section~\ref{ssec:templates}. No statistically significant excess over the Standard Model prediction is observed. For the Run~2 and Run~3 combined dataset, the observed upper limits on $\mathcal{B}_{\text{sig}}$ range from $6.3 \times 10^{-7}$ to $3.9 \times 10^{-6}$ over the unblinded mass points. The strongest observed limit is obtained for $(\mHc, \mA) = (160, 40)\GeV$, while the weakest observed limit is obtained in the on-\PZ region for $(\mHc, \mA) = (130, 90)\GeV$.

The expected upper limits for the Run~2 and Run~3 combined dataset are shown in Fig.~\ref{fig:limits}. The limits are strongest in regions where the dimuon mass peak is well separated from the \PZ boson resonance and where the signal acceptance remains high. The sensitivity is reduced in the on-\PZ region, where the ParticleNet-based selection is used to suppress the dominant $\ttbar+\PZ$ and diboson backgrounds.

This interpretation extends the previous CMS search for $\PHc \to \PW\PA$ by including the off-shell $\PW^{*}$ regime and by using the combined Run~2 and Run~3 dataset.
