\begin{abstract}
    This thesis presents a search for a light charged Higgs boson in rare top quark decays using proton-proton collision data collected by the CMS detector at the CERN LHC. The data correspond to an integrated luminosity of 138~\fbinv at 13~\TeV and 62~\fbinv at 13.6~\TeV. The search targets the decay chain $\PQt \to \PHp\PQb$, followed by $\PHp \to \PWp\PA$ and $\PA \to \Pgmp\Pgmm$, where \PHp is the charged Higgs boson and \PA is a CP-odd Higgs boson predicted by Two-Higgs-Doublet Models. The final state consists of either $\Pe\Pgm\Pgm$ or $\Pgm\Pgm\Pgm$ configuration, with at least two jets, one of which is identified as originating from a \PQb quark. The search covers charged Higgs boson masses $\mHc$ from 70 to 160\GeV and CP-odd Higgs boson masses $\mA$ from 15\GeV to $\mHc - 5\GeV$. A ParticleNet-based classifier is used to discriminate signal from \PZ-associated backgrounds, and signal is extracted using a binned maximum likelihood fit to the dimuon invariant mass distribution. No statistically significant excess over the standard model prediction is observed. The observed upper limits at 95\% confidence level on the signal branching ratio, $\mathcal{B}_{\text{sig}} = \mathcal{B}(\PQt \to \PHp\PQb) \times \mathcal{B}(\PHp \to \PWp\PA) \times \mathcal{B}(\PA \to \Pgmp\Pgmm)$, are $(0.60\text{--}2.81) \times 10^{-6}$ below the \PZ resonance, $(0.90\text{--}2.98) \times 10^{-6}$ above the \PZ resonance, and $(1.85\text{--}3.89) \times 10^{-6}$ for the classifier-optimized on-\PZ fit. This result represents the first search for the decay $\PHp \to \PWp\PA$ including off-shell effects.
\end{abstract}
